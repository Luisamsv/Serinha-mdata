Serinhaem River monthly data — data dictionary

Files
-----
T1_monthly_data.txt
  Temporal configuration T1.
  Calibration: 1966-01 through 2005-12.
  Validation: 2006-01 through 2019-04.

T2_monthly_data.txt
  Temporal configuration T2.
  Calibration: 1966-01 through 2011-12.
  Validation: 2012-01 through 2019-04.

Columns
-------
date
  Monthly observation date, represented by the first day of the month.
  Format: YYYY-MM-DD.

freshwater_discharge_m3_s
  Observed monthly river discharge at the Itubera gauging station.
  Unit: m^3 s^-1.

precipitation_mm_month
  Observed monthly precipitation at the Camamu rain gauge.
  Unit: mm month^-1.

data_role
  Role of the monthly observation in the corresponding temporal configuration.
  Allowed values: calibration, validation.

Missing values
--------------
NA denotes an unavailable original observation. Missing discharge and
precipitation values are retained at their original monthly positions and are
not imputed. Lagged predictors associated with an unavailable observation also
remain unavailable.

Internal model notation
-----------------------
Qk denotes freshwater discharge lagged by k months.
Pk denotes precipitation lagged by k months.
These are model-predictor labels generated within the analysis script and are
not raw-data column names.
